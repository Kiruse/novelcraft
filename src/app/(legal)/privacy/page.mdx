# Privacy Policy

## Introduction
*NovelCraft* ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect
We do not directly collect or store any personally identifying information from users. Instead, we rely on third-party services below to handle user authentication, data management, and billing. We collect anonymized data required for the use of this platform. We do not collect telemetry data. We do not use cookies beyond utility for the functioning of the platform, or other tracking technologies.

### Clerk
[Clerk](https://clerk.com) is an identity provider for user authentication and management services. Clerk may collect and process your personal information in accordance with their own privacy policy.

### Stripe
[Stripe](https://stripe.com) is a billing and payment service provider. Stripe may collect and process your personal information in accordance with their own privacy policy and in compliance with local laws.

## How We Use Your Information
We do not use or store your personal data. All data directly collected on this platform is anonymized.

In your use of this platform, you will engage with [Large Language Models (LLMs)](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/large-language-models). Your prompts are anonymized and supplied to [OpenRouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/) for the purpose of generating LLM responses required for the use of this platform. Your prompts are stored in our database for the functioning of this service.

In your use of this platform, you may selectively choose to share your personalized content with others. Such content may be made available to other users of this platform at your discretion, but is otherwise kept confidential.

## Data Sharing
We do not share your personal data with third parties. In your use of this platform, you will engage with Large Language Models (LLMs) and other related AI services, deferred through [OpenRouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/), which receive and process your anonymized prompts. Beware that third parties may be able to infer your identify from your prompts. You are strongly advised to not share any sensitive information on this platform.

## Data Retention
We do not retain any personal information directly. Data retention policies are handled by our third-party providers (Stripe and Clerk) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

We may retain anonymized data collected from your use of this platform for the purpose of improving our services.

## Your Rights

If you wish to have your data removed or have questions about your personal information, you must contact the respective service providers. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.

- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy)
- [Clerk](https://clerk.com/privacy)

We cannot remove data that is stored by these third-party services, as we do not have direct access to or control over their data storage systems. Beware that local regulations may require certain data to be retained regardless of your deletion request to prevent fraud, abuse, money laundering, and other criminal activities.

Any of your data we hold can be deleted by you at any time. Where applicable, data can be deleted directly in the application. Shared content can be made private at any time directly within the application. Deletion of your account will result in the removal of all your data, including shared content, from our servers after a 30 day grace period.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us through our [support page](/support), or send us an email to [privacy@novelcraft.quest](mailto:privacy@novelcraft.quest).
